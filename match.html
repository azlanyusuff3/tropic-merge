How to Play

Swap two neighbouring monsters. Match 3 or more of the same monster in a row or column to earn points.

★

Match 4 creates a line-blast monster. Swap it to clear a whole row or column.

✨

Match 5+ evolves a monster! It stays on the board. Swap it with a neighbour to blast a 3×3 area.

💥

Two evolved monsters swapped together trigger a 5×5 MEGA BLAST.

✦
