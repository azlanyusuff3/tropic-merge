How to Play

Swap two neighbouring monsters. Match 3 or more of the same monster in a row or column to earn points.

★

Every Match 3+ makes the matching monsters cast their element first: slime, water, puff, leaves, rocks or ice. These are visual effects and do not remove extra tiles.

★

Match 4 casts a stronger elemental burst and creates a line-blast monster.

✨

Match 5 evolves a monster; 6 creates an Enhanced monster; 7+ creates a Super monster with a stronger attack. Swap it with a neighbour to unleash its element.

💥

Five evolved powers: Bubblo sweeps two rows; Shadebub explodes 3×3; Groveling clears diagonals; Lumiorb removes the swapped type; Infernodon blasts a row and column.

⭐

Two evolved monsters combine into a 5×5 MEGA BLAST. Tap an evolved monster to preview its power; drag for an exact target preview.

✦

T or L of 5+ also creates an evolved monster, not a star. Matches caused by falling monsters are automatic chain reactions.

Six base monsters evolve into five special characters. No timer, lives or move limit. Your board and best score save automatically.

Start fresh?

Your current board and score will reset. Your best score stays safe.
